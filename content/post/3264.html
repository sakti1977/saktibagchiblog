We often complain that people don’t understand us. We have good intentions but people misunderstand us. People going by the look of it but miss the core intention.

Do not leave a lot for people to interpret unless you want to confuse trick them. Tell your intentions upfront. Your job will be easy and listeners job will be much easier.

A sweet smile, lots of sweet words but telling a harsh thing irritates people. They understand your smile and words were fake.

Using rough words and a flat face but telling something positive, creates more confusion than happiness.

Do not rely on your body language to convey your intention, declare it upfront.
