One occasion when our expectations are at boiling point if we do not get what we want we lose trust on the person.

One occasion when we see our needs are at boiling point if we take the uncomfortable step we gain confidence for lifetime.

One occasion when our emotions are at boiling point if we get the supporting hand and gentle words of solace we consider that person as our Best Friend Forever.

But..

If we miss to attempt at the boiling point it takes years to get that.

Be watchful of the boiling point and act !
