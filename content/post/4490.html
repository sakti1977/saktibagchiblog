I was asked by a friend in my 16th year at my current company, “How did you manage work-life balance?”

The first thing that came to mind when I heard this question was the beautiful flower pot my team presented me with for my birthday.

The family is the flower pot that keeps us sane, beautiful, and acceptable even as we flourish in our professional environment, which is like the soil, the air, and the water.

It’s analogous to maintaining work and personal life the way you maintain soil and a flowerpot.
