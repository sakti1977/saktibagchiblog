It’s not long when we all encountered lockdown, pandemic, and the greatest threat to the human race for the first time. We all were scared and thought twice before going out, changed our food habits, and questioned our need for materialistic pleasures. The world cried collectively ‘A New Normal.’ But today, when you step out, you can see the craving for the old normal is so much it feels we are celebrating the miscarriage of beautiful thoughts that developed over the past 9 months
