Our life is mostly governed by some micro decisions we take every moment. These decisions are always to choose one among two or more options. When have more things to loose and less things to gain from a bold decision and vice a versa for a cautious decision. I have seen people taking bold decisions and loose. I have also seen people taking cautious decisions and loose as well. When you loose, whatever decision you take is considered as wrong by majority of people. When you win, everybody believes that it was a right decision.  So decisions are considered as right or wrong based on the end result.

So, should we be taking the decision thinking whether the end result is going to be good or bad? Is that the only criteria behind taking decision? Can we take decisions detaching it from the results?
