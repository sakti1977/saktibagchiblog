You constantly juggle between many priorities. Every moment we are at a spot to chose one between many. The tasks do not come with a price tag but they are expensive. Some might take your time and some may peace of mind, some have the ability to make you rich and some will make you wise.

Choose the one you find worth based on your life goal.

Scolding an annoying person may tempt you but will take your peace of mind and time.

Helping a needy may look like time costly but will give you satisfaction and make you upbeat.

Likes and comments can wait but a new idea to do your job better is volatile.

Person seating next to you may feel better if you chat for a while than you attending a boring or unproductive meeting.

Choose your battle wisely. Chose your priorities wisely.
