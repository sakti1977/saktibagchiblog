Ever feel like your energy on social media is going up and down like a roller coaster? Many people feel worn out after scrolling. Others have trouble keeping up with all the changes. If you don’t know how to use social media effectively, it can cause a range of emotions. As long as you use it intentionally, you can stay inspired. You can also make new friends and grow.

So, how can you make the most of social media without getting too exhausted to use it? The important thing is to deal with it in a fair and deliberate way. I’ve been trying out different ways to use social media to fit my own needs for years. Along the way, I’ve found a few simple but useful hacks.

No matter where you are in your personal or work life, this is the best way to show up online as yourself:

Feel like you have no drive?

Don’t lie. ✨ It’s okay to talk about the messy parts, the challenging times, or the things you’re learning right now. Being vulnerable is a superpower, and being honest with your viewers can help you connect with them more deeply. Being real is much more motivating than being perfect. Your motivation will go up because of the responses.

Running on a high of drive?

It’s time to act! 💀 You should use this energy boost to do something you’ve been putting off, like starting that online course. Sharing your goal with the public will hold you accountable. A new learning promise will help you stay on track. The pressure to reach your goal enforces this until you keep your promise.

Energized to the max?

You should make videos! 🎥 Video is a wonderful way to share your interest. It can be a quick tip, an insight, or a look at your work life behind the scenes. Now is your chance to move people and connect with them on a personal level. The only time you feel brave enough to write this, so enjoy it.

Not having much energy?

Engage in a useful way. 💬 Showing up doesn’t always mean making something; sometimes it just means engaging. Take the time to read, like, and share things that speak to you. You do not want to post anything yourself. But, a kind message or share can help build a community where people support each other. It will make you feel positive to share, be thankful, and praise other people.

Having trouble with your confidence?

Have fun with your wins! 🎉 Don’t be afraid to show off your growth, whether it’s a small personal goal or a big business one. Share it if you’ve finished a course, gotten over a fear, or met a goal! Your story inspire someone you don’t know. The compliments and thanks you’ll get will make you feel better about yourself.

Boosting Your Confidence?

Now is the time to motivate other people. Talk to a coworker, mentor, or friend. Share their motivating story. Sharing ideas from influential people shows trust and strengthens your business network. You raise yourself by raising others. Socializing with other people will show you how much you need to improve and learn, so check your confidence.

Your work story is unique. Be yourself, whether you’re making something or chatting. If you want to connect, learn, and grow on your own time, social media can help. It provides a useful way to do those things.

Now you ask why I am writing this blog. The answer is I am lacking the social connection. I have been busy with various activities. This has enabled me to get feedback from my friends and family. Selfish, isn’t it? But that’s what I need; then why not seek the same?
