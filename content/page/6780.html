AI is easier to understand as a stack of layers than as a list of buzzwords. Each layer builds on the ones beneath it: models are trained on data, language models turn that training into fluent text, retrieval and tools connect them to real knowledge, and evaluation and governance keep the whole thing trustworthy.

This page is the map. Every concept gets a plain-language definition, and wherever I have written an in-depth guide, the concept links straight to it. New guides will be added here as they are published.

One principle across every layer

Most AI failures are not model failures. They start in the data, the retrieval, the instructions or the missing checks around the model. Understanding the layers is what lets you find the real cause, instead of reaching for a bigger model.
