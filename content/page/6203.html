What I do

Engineering leadership

With over 25 years in healthcare IT, I have built systems that US hospitals depend on, from Revenue Cycle Management to Electronic Health Records. As Senior Development Manager at Altera Digital Health, I lead cross-geographic engineering teams across India and the US. I don’t just build systems; I build the teams and cultures that sustain them.

Writing and thought leadership

My 2018 book How to Manage Your Manager has guided young professionals through the unwritten rules of the workplace. Today I write on this blog, on Medium and in my newsletter The Tech Strategist’s Edge, from RAG architectures and AI coding to cross-cultural team dynamics. My second book, a novel, is in progress.

Speaking and mentoring

As Past President of the IIMB Orators Toastmasters Club, public speaking is both passion and platform, from technical keynotes to the philosophy of leadership. Over the years I have also mentored many professionals through career transitions: I listen deeply, ask hard questions and help people find their own answers.

The writer

“The greatest university we are enrolled in is Life. I am simply trying to learn something from this institution, and share the notes.”

I began writing to express myself. Somewhere along the way I discovered that the most personal stories are also the most universal ones. A barefoot walk on a Kerala beach became a meditation on healing. A parking dispute became a window into the nature of human conflict. A conversation with my own inner voice became a map back to mental peace.

My writing draws from two rivers that run through my life in parallel: the ancient and the contemporary. I reach for the Purusharthas and Ashrama Dharma when modern language falls short. I hear Ghalib on a morning drive through the Kerala backwaters. I see Ganesha not as mythology but as a blueprint for leadership that still holds. The other river is honest self-examination.

That is what this blog is: not a platform, not a brand, but a journal kept in public, for anyone who has felt the gap between the life they are living and the life they sense is possible. I do not write to impress. I write to stay honest.
